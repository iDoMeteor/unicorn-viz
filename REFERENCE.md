## Purpose

This is the top-level reference for the Unicorn Viz marketing and launch plan.
It explains what the major pieces are, the basic launch strategy, and where to
find the detailed working documents.

## Basic Plan

The launch is organized around a simple priority stack:

1. Show first.
2. Software second.
3. Everything else supports those two goals.

The current campaign structure is:

- 30 days of pre-launch rollout.
- 6 live premiere days.
- 60 days of post-launch momentum.

The marketing system is intentionally split into short rollout docs, expanded
prompt/copy packs, and a scalable asset hierarchy so work can be produced and
reused over the long term.
