# Influencer and DJ Outreach Plan

Owner: Studio Marketing
Status: active
Last updated: 2026-06-16

## Purpose

Build a repeatable outreach lane for influencers and DJs who can amplify the premiere before launch day and sustain replay momentum after launch.

## Core Objectives

- Drive pre-premiere awareness through trusted creator and DJ voices.
- Secure reposts, reaction clips, story mentions, and playlist/set support.
- Create relationship continuity through launch week and post-launch follow-ups.

## Timeline

### T-30 to T-21: Build and Score List

- Build a target list across short-form creators, visual artists, streamers, and DJs.
- Score each target on fit, reach, responsiveness, and platform overlap.
- Prioritize creators aligned with electronic, experimental, VJ, and underground scenes.

### T-20 to T-15: Warm Touches

- Follow, engage, and leave relevant comments before direct outreach.
- Log early interactions so first direct messages are not cold.
- Tag each contact by preferred channel: email, Instagram DM, TikTok DM, or X DM.

### T-14 to T-08: Primary Outreach Wave

- Send first-touch outreach using the template pack.
- Focus first on Tier 1 and Tier 2 contacts.
- Log outcomes daily and redirect quickly to alternates if no response.

### T-07 to T-02: Follow-Ups and Confirmations

- Send follow-up 1 to non-responders.
- Confirm posting windows for positive replies.
- Offer easy-use assets: prewritten captions, clips, and premiere links.

### T-01 to Day 02: Premiere Push

- Send same-day reminder packets to confirmed collaborators.
- Repost collaborator content rapidly.
- Track uplift by post type and collaborator tier.

### Day 03 to Day 14: Post-Premiere Retention

- Send thank-you messages and share performance highlights.
- Ask high-performing collaborators for second-wave posts.
- Convert best contacts to ongoing partners for future drops.

## Contact Segments

- Tier 1: High-fit, high-credibility creators and DJs with proven electronic/experimental engagement.
- Tier 2: Mid-size creators with high audience interaction and format fit.
- Tier 3: Emerging creators and local DJs with strong scene authenticity.

## Minimum Data Fields Per Contact

- Contact name
- Handle/channel
- Role (influencer, DJ, both)
- Primary platform
- Region/timezone
- Audience estimate
- Fit notes
- Contact route
- First touch date
- Status
- Follow-up dates
- Result and link

## Offer Structure

Use one clear ask per outreach message.

- Share request: story/reel/post featuring track + visual preview.
- DJ support request: include track in upcoming set, stream, or show mention.
- Premiere request: mention or link on premiere day with tracked URL.

## Success Targets (First 2 Weeks)

- 60 to 80 total outbound contacts.
- 20% to 30% response rate.
- 10 to 20 collaborator posts before Day 2.
- At least 5 DJ play/support confirmations.

## Daily Operating Rhythm

- 30 to 45 minutes list maintenance.
- 45 to 60 minutes outreach sends.
- 20 minutes response handling.
- 20 minutes analytics and next-day reprioritization.

## Risk Controls

- Avoid spam bursts by spacing sends in blocks.
- Do not send identical text to all contacts.
- Keep asks low-friction and single-action.
- Always provide direct links and one-sentence context.
