# Social Ops Stack Recommendation

Owner: Studio Marketing
Status: draft
Last updated: 2026-06-09

## Purpose

Capture the short-list of tools that can manage content across the expanded platform mix without turning launch ops into tab chaos.

## Recommended Stack

If we keep this to two services, use:

1. Metricool as the primary planning, scheduling, and analytics hub.
2. Publer as the secondary publishing and account-breadth hub.

## Why Metricool First

- Strong fit for Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest, and other brand channels.
- Free plan supports one brand and automatic publishing for a small starter cadence.
- Paid plans add broader brand management, campaign dashboards, analytics history, and Looker Studio connector support.
- Good match for the scoreboard/dashboard concept because reporting and campaign tracking are built in.

## Why Publer Second

- Free plan supports three social accounts and covers Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Threads, Pinterest, and more, with X excluded on free.
- Paid plans add unlimited scheduled posts, approval workflows, first-comment scheduling, and a more flexible queue.
- Good fallback if we want a simple content factory for short-form, repurposed posts, and reusable templates.

## When Buffer Makes Sense

Use Buffer only if X becomes the main bottleneck and we want a very simple composer/scheduler with a small account count.

- Free plan supports 3 channels.
- It covers Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X, and other major channels.
- It is clean and lightweight, but it is not the broadest fit for a large multi-platform launch on the free tier.

## Operating Split

- Metricool owns analytics, campaign dashboards, publishing plan, and weekly performance review.
- Publer owns reusable queueing, drafts, and fast repurposing of vertical content.
- The scoreboard sheet should stay the manual source of truth until the dashboards are wired.

## Immediate Setup Priority

1. Connect the official FB, IG, TikTok, YouTube, and X profiles.
2. Build one campaign named for the 30-day runway.
3. Define one CTA per post type before scheduling.
4. Sync the scoreboard fields to the weekly review template.
