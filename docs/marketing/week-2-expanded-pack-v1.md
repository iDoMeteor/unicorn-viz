- Primary slot: local evening (hero assets).
- Secondary slot: local midday (support assets).
- Story/note slot: late morning.

- First 2 seconds are visually strong.
- One CTA only.
- Links are tested.
- Caption first line is platform-specific.
- Backup asset is ready.
