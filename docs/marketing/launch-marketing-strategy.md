# Unicorn Viz x DJ Unicorn Tears Launch Marketing Strategy

Owner: Studio Marketing
Status: draft
Last updated: 2026-05-24

## Mission

Launch Unicorn Viz as both:

1. A world-class live visual performance system.
2. A creator ecosystem around DJ Unicorn Tears (music, visuals, merch, and digital products).

The launch goal is not just awareness. The goal is sustained audience growth plus recurring revenue across music, software, and direct fan support.

## Brand Positioning

### Core Position

Unicorn Viz is the live visual instrument for the new underground-to-mainstream electronic era.

### Artist Position

DJ Unicorn Tears is the flagship world-premiere artist: house/EDM/techno energy, cinematic visual identity, and original performance storytelling.

### Tagline Direction (working)

- Sound You Feel. Visuals You Wear.
- Future Club Energy, Engineered.
- The Beat Has A New Skin.

## Launch Architecture

## 1. Product Stack

- Free layer:
  - Free starter visual packs
  - Free DJ Unicorn Tears single/teaser clips
  - Free social cutdowns and short loops
- Paid digital layer:
  - Premium effect packs and drop-ins
  - Premium visual themes, overlays, and show presets
  - Sample packs and exclusive extended mixes
- Physical layer:
  - Posters, hoodies, apparel capsules
  - Limited premiere run items
- Membership layer:
  - Patron tiers with early drops, behind-the-scenes, private livestreams

## 2. Channel Stack

Primary public channels:

- YouTube
- Spotify
- Rumble
- X
- Odysee
- Brighteon

Community and monetization channels:

- Patreon
- Substack
- SubscribeStar
- Locals

Live and streaming channels:

- Twitch
- Kick

Website and commerce:

- Preferred: custom website with embedded Printful plus native digital product checkout
- Optional interim bridge: Fourthwall for rapid storefront launch while custom stack matures

## 3. Website Strategy

### Recommendation

Go custom site as the canonical home, then integrate:

- Printful for merch fulfillment
- Digital product checkout for downloads
- Email capture and campaign automations
- Link hub and launch countdown

### Why custom over pure Fourthwall

- Better ownership of audience and funnel analytics
- Better SEO and long-term brand equity
- Better integration with Unicorn Viz product docs, download center, and membership flows
- Cleaner visual language for premium positioning

### Suggested site sections

- Home
- World Premiere Event
- Music
- Visual Packs
- Merch
- Downloads (Free and Pro)
- Membership
- Press Kit
- About

## Independent Artist Distribution Plan

To publish as an independent artist to major music platforms, use a distributor.

### Recommended distributors to evaluate immediately

- DistroKid
- TuneCore
- CD Baby
- Ditto
- AWAL (selective)

### Decision criteria

- Revenue share and annual fees
- Speed to platform delivery
- ISRC handling
- Content ID options
- Splits and collaborator tooling
- Pre-save and campaign features

### Action plan

1. Select distributor this week.
2. Register artist profiles on Spotify for Artists, YouTube Official Artist Channel, and platform equivalents.
3. Upload first release assets (audio masters, artwork, metadata, release date).
4. Schedule premiere release and pre-save campaign.

## Campaign Phases

## Phase 1 - Build (T minus 30 to 7 days)

Objectives:

- Build anticipation and mailing list.
- Establish DJ Unicorn Tears visual identity.
- Seed previews across all channels.

Deliverables:

- Brand reveal kit and teaser trailer
- Landing page with countdown and email capture
- Platform profile setup and link consistency
- Press and creator outreach list

## Phase 2 - Premiere Week (T minus 7 to plus 7 days)

Objectives:

- Maximize first-wave visibility.
- Convert interest to follows, downloads, and purchases.

Deliverables:

- World premiere livestream event
- Daily content cadence (short clips, behind-the-scenes, teaser cuts)
- Product launch bundle:
  - Free starter pack
  - Paid premiere pack
  - Premiere merch capsule
- Launch PR packet and outreach follow-ups

## Phase 3 - Momentum (Day 8 to Day 90)

Objectives:

- Sustain growth and monetize recurring demand.
- Turn premiere audience into repeat customers and members.

Deliverables:

- Weekly release cadence (music drops, visual packs, clips)
- Monthly limited merch drops
- Biweekly livestream sets (Twitch/Kick/YouTube)
- Partner remixes and collabs
- Structured email and membership funnel

## Content Engine

## Core content pillars

- Performance: live DJ sets + Unicorn Viz visuals
- Craft: behind-the-scenes build process
- Product: effect pack showcases and tutorials
- Culture: short-form artist narrative and scene identity

## Cadence baseline

- Shorts/Reels: 4-6 per week
- Long-form set or feature video: 1 per week
- Livestream: 1-2 per week
- Email newsletter: 1 per week

## Monetization Map

- Music streaming revenue
- Digital downloads (effect packs and bundles)
- Merch margin from Printful products
- Membership subscriptions
- Sponsorships and brand collabs
- Affiliate and tool partnerships

## KPI Framework

Track weekly and monthly:

- Audience growth:
  - Followers/subscribers per channel
  - Email list growth
- Engagement:
  - Watch time
  - Save/share rates
  - Click-through rate
- Conversion:
  - Free-to-paid conversion
  - Merch conversion rate
  - Membership conversion rate
- Revenue:
  - Revenue by channel and product class
  - Recurring revenue share vs one-off sales

## Operating Roles

- Creative Director: brand voice, visuals, release narrative
- Artist Lead: DJ Unicorn Tears performance and audience voice
- Growth Lead: paid/organic channel growth and analytics
- Product Lead: Unicorn Viz releases, docs, and launch bundles
- Commerce Lead: site, checkout, merch ops, fulfillment

## Risks and Mitigations

- Platform dependency risk:
  - Mitigation: diversify channels and capture email early
- Launch overload risk:
  - Mitigation: phased release plan and weekly sprint board
- Brand inconsistency risk:
  - Mitigation: shared style guide and creative review gate
- Conversion leakage risk:
  - Mitigation: simplify funnel and standardize CTA architecture
