# Japan and South Korea Market Strategy

Owner: Studio Marketing
Status: active
Last updated: 2026-06-18

## Purpose

Create a distinct expansion strategy for Japan and South Korea that preserves the core launch identity while adapting execution to local platforms, language expectations, and audience behavior.

## Strategic Goals

1. Build local audience trust through language-native content and consistent posting windows.
2. Grow watch, replay, and follow momentum on region-preferred platforms.
3. Convert interest into stream attendance, software downloads, and paid expansion products.
4. Establish local creator and DJ partnerships that can repeat across future releases.

## Market Positioning

### Shared Positioning

- Show first, software second.
- DJ Unicorn Tears is the emotional entry point.
- Unicorn Viz is presented as the tool behind the performance layer.

### Japan Emphasis

- Precision, craft, and visual design quality.
- Electronic and visual-performance identity with strong replay value.
- Consistent cadence and highly polished assets.

### South Korea Emphasis

- High-tempo short-form hooks and creator collaboration.
- Strong social proof loops through reposts, clips, and community channels.
- Fast release cadence and event-style moments.

## Platform Strategy by Market

## Japan Priority Stack

- YouTube: long-form live sets, replay chapters, and feature cuts.
- X: countdowns, fast updates, and collaborative repost loops.
- Instagram: reels and stories with visual-first clips.
- TikTok: high-hook vertical edits for discovery.
- Line OpenChat or Line community path: launch alerts and community retention.

## South Korea Priority Stack

- YouTube: hero long-form and replay chapters.
- Instagram: reels and story momentum with creator reposts.
- TikTok: aggressive discovery and clip testing.
- Naver ecosystem path: discoverability support via local web and blog surfaces.
- Local live-community channel support: region-native creator communities and stream discovery channels.

## Language and Localization System

## Minimum Localization Set Per Asset

- Japanese headline and caption variant.
- Korean headline and caption variant.
- Subtitle versions for both languages on hero clips and trailers.
- Localized call to action links and landing paths.

## Voice Rules

- Avoid direct translation only; adapt for cultural tone and rhythm.
- Keep lines short and concrete.
- Maintain artist identity while localizing phrase structure.
- Use local date and time formatting for every stream announcement.

## Posting Windows

- Japan prime windows: 19:00 to 23:00 JST.
- South Korea prime windows: 19:00 to 23:00 KST.
- Use secondary daytime test windows for short-form discovery.
- Publish schedule cards with both JST and KST side by side.

## 90-Day Rollout

## Phase 1: Foundation and Seeding (Day 1 to Day 30)

Goals:

- Build local profile readiness and first awareness wave.

Actions:

- Localize bios, profile headers, and pinned posts for JP and KR variants.
- Build localized links pages for watch, replay, download, and support.
- Publish first 20 to 30 localized short-form clips split between music-first and visual-first hooks.
- Start outreach to Japanese and Korean creators, DJs, and visual curators.
- Run one localization QA pass per week on subtitles, titles, and landing pages.

## Phase 2: Activation and Partnerships (Day 31 to Day 60)

Goals:

- Convert attention into collaborative reach and live attendance.

Actions:

- Run weekly collaboration slots with local creators or DJs.
- Release market-specific event cards and countdown clips.
- Launch one JP-focused and one KR-focused stream night with tailored intros and callouts.
- Publish replay highlight packs within 6 hours after each stream.
- Promote the free core software path with local-language how-to clips.

## Phase 3: Conversion and Retention (Day 61 to Day 90)

Goals:

- Turn recurring viewers into software users and paying supporters.

Actions:

- Launch localized premium bundle pages with clear feature framing.
- Add member or supporter perks with localized descriptions.
- Run weekly recap posts that include one direct next-step call to action.
- Segment email and community lists by market and by interest (music, visuals, software).
- Prepare a second 90-day cycle based on winning formats and creators.

## Content Mix by Market

## Japan Weekly Mix

- 2 long-form videos or replay chapters.
- 10 to 14 short-form clips.
- 3 to 5 X update posts tied to stream schedule and highlights.
- 1 community post with upcoming stream details.

## South Korea Weekly Mix

- 2 long-form videos or replay chapters.
- 12 to 16 short-form clips with fast hook testing.
- 4 to 6 Instagram and TikTok momentum posts.
- 1 to 2 collaboration posts with local creators.

## Partnership Strategy

## Partner Types

- Electronic DJs with active short-form presence.
- Visual creators and motion artists.
- Livestream and replay commentary creators.
- Electronic culture pages and curators.

## Outreach Structure

- First-touch message in local language.
- One clear collaboration ask per outreach.
- 3-5 day follow-up cycle.
- Shared asset pack: short clips, caption options, link set, and timing notes.

## Funnel Mapping (JP and KR)

1. Discovery: short-form clips and creator reposts.
2. Capture: localized links page and follow prompts.
3. Conversion: watch live, download free core, or purchase premium packs.
4. Retention: replay clips, community updates, and recurring stream cadence.

## Metrics and Decision Gates

## Core Metrics

- Reach: impressions, unique viewers, and profile visits by market.
- Engagement: watch time, completion rates, saves, shares, and comments.
- Capture: follows, email signups, and community joins by market.
- Conversion: free downloads, premium purchases, and support actions.
- Partner impact: repost rate, collaboration outputs, and referral traffic.

## Decision Gates

- If reach is high and conversion is low: simplify localized calls to action and landing flow.
- If short-form performs and long-form lags: increase replay chapter density.
- If one market outperforms by more than 30 percent: keep both active but shift asset volume toward the winning market while preserving minimum baseline cadence in the other.

## Operating Requirements

- One owner for localization quality.
- One owner for partner outreach and tracking.
- One owner for regional analytics reporting.
- Shared calendar that always shows JST and KST.
- Weekly review meeting focused on market-specific learning.

## Risks and Controls

- Risk: Over-translation that weakens tone.
  Control: Local adaptation review before publish.

- Risk: Inconsistent posting windows.
  Control: Fixed market schedules and pre-scheduled assets.

- Risk: Partnership drop-off.
  Control: Keep backup partner list and rotate asks.

- Risk: Funnel friction for non-English users.
  Control: Localized links pages and simplified conversion paths.
