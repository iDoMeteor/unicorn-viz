# College Radio Outreach Email Pack

Owner: Studio Marketing
Status: active
Last updated: 2026-06-16

## Purpose

Provide ready-to-send outreach templates for college radio stations during the pre-premiere window.

## Send Timing

- Initial outreach: around T-14.
- Follow-up 1: 3 to 4 days after initial email if no reply.
- Follow-up 2: 4 to 5 days after follow-up 1 if no reply.

## Response Reply Snippets

## If they ask for a downloadable file

Thanks for the quick reply. Here is a download link:
{{song_link}}
If you need WAV/clean/radio edit metadata, I can send that too.

## If they ask for broadcast metadata

Happy to send details.

- Artist: {{artist_name}}
- Title: {{track_title}}
- Genre: electronic / indie
- Release date: {{premiere_date}}
- Links: {{song_link}}

## If they pass

Thanks for the reply and for listening. I appreciate your time.
If timing changes later, I would be glad to resubmit future releases.

## Quality Checklist Before Sending

- Subject line is specific and not spammy.
- First line references station fit.
- Exactly one clear request.
- All links are tested.
- Station name and recipient name are correct.
- Tone is short, human, and respectful.

## Notes

- Keep initial email under 140 words whenever possible.
- Do not send mass BCC blasts from one thread; use personalized sends by tier.
- Track every send and reply in the outreach sheet.
