Owner: Studio Marketing
Status: active
Last updated: 2026-06-18

## Purpose

Provide a practical first 14-day publishing schedule for Japan and South Korea expansion work, with clear daily output and platform focus.

## Operating Rules

- Show first, software second.
- Publish one hero item and one support item per market per day.
- Use local posting windows: 19:00 to 23:00 JST and 19:00 to 23:00 KST.
- Include one CTA per post.

## CTA Rotation

- Follow channel
- Download free core
