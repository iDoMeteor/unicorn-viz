Owner: Studio Marketing
Status: active

## Purpose

Provide reusable outreach templates for Japanese and South Korean creators, DJs, curators, and media channels.

## Localization Rules

- Send first touch in the contact's preferred language when possible.
- Keep short-form messages under 120 words.
- Use local time labels in CTAs: JST for Japan, KST for South Korea.
- Keep one CTA per message.

## JP Lean Variant (Tone Guide)

Use this style for Japan-targeted contacts:

- More formal and concise tone.
- Emphasize quality, craft, and consistency.
- Avoid slang and over-hype wording.

Starter opener:

Thank you for your work and curation. I am sharing this in case it is a fit for your audience.

## KR Lean Variant (Tone Guide)

Use this style for South Korea-targeted contacts:

- Fast, clear, high-energy opening.
- Emphasize short-form performance and collaboration value.
- Keep CTA direct and practical.

Starter opener:

I am sharing a high-impact electronic visual release that may fit your audience and posting style.

## CTA Menu

- Story post with track and premiere link.
- Reel/Short featuring 15 to 30 second preview.
- Set/show mention during release week.
- Community repost with local-time premiere reminder.
- Replay-share post after stream ends.
