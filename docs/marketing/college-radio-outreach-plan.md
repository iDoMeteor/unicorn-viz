# College Radio Outreach Plan

Owner: Studio Marketing
Status: draft
Last updated: 2026-06-09

## Mission

Build a college-radio contact pool during the launch runway, then begin direct outreach about 14 days before the big premiere with songs, visuals, and a concise pitch.

## Why This Matters

- College radio gives us a music-first discovery lane that fits DJ Unicorn Tears.
- It also gives Unicorn Viz a credibility lane with student programmers, indie shows, and campus tastemakers.
- The approach works best when the list is gathered early and the pitch lands close to premiere.

## Timeline

### T-30 to T-15

- Gather station data.
- Group stations by region, format, and fit.
- Find each station's submission page, music director contact, and general inbox.
- Build a clean mailing/pitch sheet.

### T-14 to Premiere

- Begin the first outreach wave.
- Send songs, a visual preview, and the premiere note.
- Follow up with top-fit stations that respond.
- Track every send, reply, and pass.

### Premiere Window

- Re-send the strongest pitch only to the best responders.
- Share replay clips, not just the live link.
- Convert any active interest into interviews, spins, or shoutouts.

## Target Data Fields

Use one row per station.

- Station call sign
- Station name
- School or parent institution
- City
- State
- Frequency or stream URL
- Format or genre note
- Music director or program director contact
- General station email
- Submission page URL
- Best contact method
- Pitch status
- Follow-up date
- Notes

## Outreach Packet Contents

- 1 short artist bio
- 1 short software bio
- 1 premiere date note
- 1 song link
- 1 visual preview link
- 1 download link for Unicorn Viz core
- 1 simple request: play, share, or reply with the best contact

## Response Tracking

- New
- Contacted
- Replied
- Sent to music director
- Sent to programmer
- Passed
- Played / scheduled
- Interview invite

## Notes

- Keep the pitch short and human.
- Prioritize fit over volume.
- Remove any station from the target list if the submission path is unclear or the station is inactive.
- Use the college-radio wave to support the premiere, not to distract from the core launch narrative.
