## Tone Modes

Choose one mode per contact.

### Mode 1: Professional

Best for larger creators, label-adjacent contacts, and formal radio/show workflows.

- Keep concise and structured.
- Use bullets for links.
- Keep ask singular and specific.

### Mode 2: Creator Casual

Best for peer-level creators and student-facing channels.

- Use conversational wording.
- Keep message under 120 words.
- Ask for one lightweight action.

### Mode 3: Ultra-Short

Best for high-volume DMs and low-attention inboxes.

- Keep to 4 to 6 lines.
- Put links immediately after first sentence.
- Remove any non-essential context.

## Common CTA Options

- Post a 15 to 30 second story with track + premiere link.
- Include track in your next livestream or set recap.
- Repost the visual preview with your caption.
- Mention premiere date in your next post block.
- Share track in your Discord or community feed.
