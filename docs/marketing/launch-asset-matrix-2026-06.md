# DJ Unicorn Tears x Unicorn Viz Launch Asset Matrix

Owner: Studio Marketing
Status: active
Last updated: 2026-06-03

## Purpose

This matrix translates strategy into production-ready assets for the 30-day
countdown and six-day world premiere run. It defines:

- Platform-specific post formats.
- Primary calls to action by platform and objective.
- A practical 14-day publishing prompt bank for immediate execution.

## Asset Taxonomy

Use consistent naming so clips, thumbnails, and copy are easy to track.

- `HERO`: flagship trailers, event promos, major reveal edits.
- `PROOF`: product demos, free-vs-paid comparisons, workflow clips.
- `HYPE`: short high-energy highlights, countdown cuts, reactions.
- `STORY`: artist voice, founder letters, behind-the-scenes.
- `COMMUNITY`: polls, Q&A prompts, supporter shout-outs, fan features.
- `OFFER`: merch cards, premium drop-in highlights, bundle explainers.
- `SITE_NOTE`: small website-only support note for the 10% animals-in-need message.

## CTA Library

Each post should use one primary CTA only. Reuse these lines as approved
defaults and tailor lightly per platform tone.

### Discovery CTAs

- "Watch the world premiere live."
- "Follow DJ Unicorn Tears for daily countdown drops."
- "Set your reminder for Day 1."

### Capture CTAs

- "Join the premiere list for instant alerts and drops."
- "Subscribe for behind-the-scenes launch updates."
- "Get the free launch pack when it goes live."

### Conversion CTAs

- "Download Unicorn Viz core free."
- "Unlock premium drop-ins and launch bundles."
- "Grab the premiere merch capsule before it closes."
- "Join founding supporter tiers for early access."

### Website-only support note

- "10% of proceeds help animals in need." (footer/support page usage only)

## Creative Specs Snapshot

- Vertical clip master: 1080x1920, 15 to 35 seconds.
- Short horizontal cut: 1920x1080, 30 to 75 seconds.
- Hero trailer: 1920x1080, 45 to 120 seconds.
- Caption length target: 80 to 180 chars for social, 1 to 3 short paragraphs for Substack/Locals.
- Thumbnail package: one text-forward version, one no-text version.

## Repurposing Rules

- One hero asset should produce at least 8 derivatives: 3 vertical clips, 2
  horizontal cuts, 1 static quote card, 1 schedule card, 1 recap clip.
- Save at least 30% of the strongest visuals for D-3 through D1.
- Keep one backup clip per day in case a platform post underperforms.
- Build captions from templates but rewrite the first line per platform.

## Publishing Operations Checklist

- Confirm one primary CTA per post before scheduling.
- Verify links and tracking parameters before publish.
- Add comment hooks to posts that need engagement velocity.
- Queue same-day repost windows for major assets.
- Log daily outputs and performance in the campaign tracker.
