# Influencer and DJ Outreach Sheet Guide

Owner: Studio Marketing
Status: active
Last updated: 2026-06-16

## Purpose

Use this guide with the influencer/DJ outreach CSV to keep prospecting, sends, and follow-ups consistent during the pre-premiere runway.

## Files

- Template CSV: [influencer-dj-outreach-sheet-template.csv](influencer-dj-outreach-sheet-template.csv)
- Mission plan: [influencer-dj-outreach-plan.md](influencer-dj-outreach-plan.md)
- Message pack: [influencer-dj-outreach-message-pack.md](influencer-dj-outreach-message-pack.md)

## Setup

1. Import the CSV into Google Sheets.
2. Freeze row 1.
3. Add filters to every column.
4. Sort by `fit_score` (high to low), then by `platform`.
5. Keep one row per person/channel only.

## Status Values

Use these values in `first_touch_status`, `followup_1_status`, and `followup_2_status`:

- `not_started`
- `sent`
- `replied`
- `interested`
- `passed`
- `scheduled`
- `posted`
- `played_in_set`

## Cadence

- T-30 to T-15: build list, qualify fit, and warm touch.
- T-14 to T-8: send first outreach wave to Tier 1 and Tier 2.
- T-7 to T-4: follow up non-responders and confirm collaborator windows.
- T-3 to Day 1: send reminder packets to confirmed collaborators.
- Day 2 to Day 14: thank-you messages and second-wave asks.

## Minimum Required Before First Send

Each row should have:

- `contact_name`
- `handle_or_show_name`
- `role`
- `platform`
- `contact_route`
- `contact_url`
- one clear `cta_requested`

## Tracking Rules

- Log every outbound touch with date.
- Do not send more than 2 follow-ups before premiere.
- Set `asset_pack_sent`, `track_link_sent`, `preview_link_sent`, and `premiere_link_sent` to `yes` only when actually sent.
- Keep notes short, objective, and actionable.

## Suggested Weekly Goal

- Add 50 to 100 qualified contacts/week.
- Warm-touch at least 30 contacts/week.
- Send 25 to 40 personalized first-touch messages/week.
- Keep Tier 1 list under 40 contacts for focused execution.
