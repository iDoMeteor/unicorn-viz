# DJ Unicorn Tears World Premiere Asset Checklist

Owner: Studio Marketing
Status: active
Last updated: 2026-06-03

## Purpose

This is the operational checklist for:

- The first 15 days of countdown.
- The second 15 days of countdown.
- Each of the 6 livestream days.

This is intentionally designed for a solo creator workflow.

## Priority Order

1. Show first.
2. Software second.
3. Everything else is support.

The 10% animals-in-need note should stay as a lightweight website element,
not a repeated campaign headline.

## Grand Narrative and Sub-Arcs

### Grand narrative

DJ Unicorn Tears is opening a new live performance world where music, motion,
and visuals hit as one continuous experience.

### Sub-arc A: The signal appears (Days 1-10)

Audience should feel: curiosity and rising anticipation.

### Sub-arc B: The system reveals itself (Days 11-20)

Audience should feel: confidence that the show is real and technically sharp.

### Sub-arc C: Final ignition (Days 21-30)

Audience should feel: urgency to show up live.

### Live arc: Six-night event story (Live Days 1-6)

Audience should feel: each day is a distinct chapter, not a repeat episode.

## Global Asset Packs

Prepare these once, then reuse throughout the campaign.

- Brand core pack:
  - 2 logo lockups
  - 4 background textures/plates
  - 8 thumbnail templates
  - 4 lower-third overlays
- Sound core pack:
  - Theme song master
  - Stream intro reel full and short cuts
  - 5 stingers/transitions
- Messaging core pack:
  - 12 short caption hooks
  - 8 long caption templates
  - 10 CTA lines prioritized for watch and reminder actions
- Stream core pack:
  - 6 day title cards
  - 6 countdown cards
  - 6 replay cards

## Checklist: First 15 Days (D-30 to D-16)

Goal: establish identity, generate curiosity, and build reminder intent.

### Must-have assets

- 3 hero teasers (20 to 45 seconds).
- 12 short vertical clips (10 to 30 seconds).
- 3 founder/story posts (written or spoken).
- 2 setup behind-the-scenes clips.
- 2 schedule teaser cards (without full day-by-day breakdown yet).
- 1 countdown visual system (numbers, colorway, typography).
- 1 official watch-destination card.

### Optional high-value assets

- 1 micro-trailer cut from theme song drop moments.
- 1 creator outreach preview reel.
- 1 visual style poll card.

### First 15-day quality gate

- You can post daily for 7 days straight without creating anything new.
- You have at least 2 unreleased backups for high-importance days.
- Your top 2 visuals are still being held for late countdown.

## Checklist: Second 15 Days (D-15 to D-1)

Goal: convert anticipation into confirmed live attendance.

### Must-have assets

- 2 final trailers (one medium, one short punch version).
- 6 day-of-week stream theme cards.
- 6 platform destination cards (one per major watch path).
- 10 short vertical clips from strongest visuals.
- 4 software proof clips showing real show moments.
- 3 reminder posts for D-3, D-2, D-1.
- 2 "what to expect" posts summarizing the six-day event structure.
- 2 replay placeholder templates ready before live week starts.

### Optional high-value assets

- 1 final hype montage using fastest cuts and strongest drops.
- 1 audience reaction or social-proof compilation.

### Second 15-day quality gate

- Full six-day schedule graphics are locked.
- All main platform links are tested and pinned.
- D-1 final countdown post is fully ready 48 hours early.

## Checklist: Live Day Assets (Each of 6 Days)

Each live day should have a repeatable asset bundle so you do not improvise under pressure.

### Daily required bundle

- 1 day-title hero card.
- 1 "starting soon" clip.
- 1 go-live post graphic.
- 1 short pre-show vertical cut.
- 1 post-show highlight cut (10 to 30 seconds).
- 1 replay card and replay link post.
- 1 short text recap for Substack or site update.

### Daily optional bundle

- 1 community prompt post.
- 1 still frame quote card.
- 1 "tomorrow night" teaser.

## Six Live Days: Specific Narrative Beats

### Live Day 1: Arrival

- Asset focus: identity, scale, first-impact visuals.
- Core message: this is a world premiere event.

### Live Day 2: Build

- Asset focus: behind-the-scenes confidence and show mechanics.
- Core message: this is a real instrument, not a gimmick.

### Live Day 3: Fire

- Asset focus: speed, intensity, set momentum.
- Core message: the show escalates.

### Live Day 4: Connection

- Asset focus: audience interaction and community response.
- Core message: people are inside the world now.

### Live Day 5: Expansion

- Asset focus: software-proof plus remix culture tie-ins.
- Core message: this world keeps growing.

### Live Day 6: Finale

- Asset focus: strongest sequences, final statements, next tease.
- Core message: chapter one ends, chapter two begins.

## Solo Workflow (Keep Me Focused Mode)

Use this recurring loop each day.

1. Pick one daily objective only.
2. Produce one hero asset first.
3. Derive 2 to 4 support assets from the hero.
4. Write one strong CTA and one fallback CTA.
5. Schedule main post, then backup post.
6. Stop editing when message clarity is high, not when perfection is reached.

## Daily Timebox Template

- 35 min: select clips and define daily story.
- 60 min: cut hero asset.
- 45 min: produce derivatives.
- 25 min: captions, links, and scheduling.
- 15 min: QA on mobile and desktop.

Total: 180 minutes.

## Minimal Web Mention for 10% Cause Note

Keep this on the websites only:

- One small line in footer or support section.
- One optional line on checkout confirmation.
- No repeated campaign-wide CTA centered on this message.

## Definition of Ready (Before D-15)

- First 15-day assets complete.
- At least 30% of strongest visuals held in reserve.
- Core stream graphics and templates done.
- Final two-week production calendar pre-filled.

## Definition of Ready (Before D-1)

- All six live-day bundles are prepped.
- Replay templates are ready.
- Platform post drafts for Day 1 and Day 2 are complete.
- Backup clips exist for any stream disruption scenario.
