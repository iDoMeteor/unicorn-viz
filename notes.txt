## Post-process candidates menu (pick 2-4)

Low risk / fast wins:
1) Film grain + mild luma dither
	- look: smoother gradients, less banding
	- controls: amount, size, animated/static
2) Chromatic aberration (subtle RGB split)
	- look: punchier edges, cyber vibe
	- controls: radius, intensity, edge-only falloff
3) Scanlines + phosphor mask (lightweight CRT)
	- look: retro texture without full CRT warp
	- controls: line density, strength, bloom mix
4) Sharpen/soften pair
	- look: recover detail after heavy blur effects
	- controls: sharpen amount, clamp

Medium risk / high style:
5) Radial zoom blur (beat hit)
	- look: impact pulses from center
	- controls: center, strength, decay, beat gate
6) Lens distortion + vignette suite
	- look: camera-lens character
	- controls: barrel/pincushion, vignette radius/softness
7) Glitch slices (horizontal jitter bands)
	- look: intentional signal corruption
	- controls: chance, slice count, amplitude, chroma offset
8) Color grade LUT-lite (curves + toe/shoulder)
	- look: cinematic grade presets
	- controls: contrast, gamma, lift/gain, temperature

High risk / showcase:
9) Temporal feedback trail
	- look: ghosting echoes and motion memory
	- controls: feedback mix, decay, smear direction
10) Datamosh-lite velocity smear
	- look: aggressive digital melt on transitions/beat
	- controls: threshold, smear amount, recovery speed
11) Multi-pass bloom pyramid
	- look: premium glow and highlights
	- controls: threshold, iterations, strength
12) Heat-haze refraction field
	- look: atmospheric shimmer, rave heat
	- controls: distortion map scale, speed, intensity
